# Texture credits

Thank you to the creators whose work appears in the OrdinarySMP resource pack.

If your textures are included in this pack and your credit is missing or incorrect, please let us know so we can give you proper credit.

See [Missing texture credits](MISSING_SOURCES.md) for textures whose creators or original sources are still unknown.

Credits supplied by the pack maintainer take precedence over old embedded credits. Where only a model credit survives, that limitation is noted. Each PNG appears once below, even when multiple models use it.
